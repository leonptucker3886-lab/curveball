## 1. The request
Leon asked for a high-quality video that:
- Opens with the LeonLink logo spinning (the blue anchor at the far left of the homepage header).
- Says **"LEONLINK presents"** then **"A BATTALION PRODUCED FILM"**.
- Has ~35 seconds of still frames telling a story about a king, with a voiceover.
- Ends with a short gameplay chapter: a Mario-style 2D side-scroller.

## 2. Brand details
- **Logo:** cyan double-anchor, far left of the leonlink.net header. File `logo.png` (256×256) in GitHub repo `leonptucker3886-lab/leonlink-home`. Site runs on Vercel project `leonlink-home`.

## 3. Decisions
- Working title: **THE LAST CROWN**. Format 1920×1080 (16:9), 30 fps.
- "Steel frames" read as **still frames**. LEONLINK written as one word, as in the header.
- Narrator: **George**, warm British voice (ElevenLabs, voice ID `JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb`). Leon chose narration **with subtitles**.

## 4. Storyboard

### 0:00 — Studio intro
The cyan anchor comes out of black, turns twice on its vertical axis, slows and stops facing camera with a flash and a deep metallic hit. "LEONLINK presents" rises in (LEON white, LINK cyan), fades to "A BATTALION PRODUCED FILM", low boom, fade to black.

### 0:07 — The story (7 painted frames, film bars, slow zooms/pans, narrated + subtitled)
1. Golden castle above a sea of clouds at sunrise — *"Long ago, the kingdom of Aldermere knew only light."*
2. King Edric on his throne, crown glowing — *"At its heart sat King Edric, and the Sunstone Crown."*
3. A shadow storm pours over the castle walls, lightning — *"Then, on the longest night, the Shadow came."*
4. A shadow claw rips the crown away in the dark throne room — *"It took the crown, and every light went with it."*
5. The king kneels alone in the rain among ruins, broken sword — *"Alone in the ashes, he could have stayed down."*
6. Sunrise; he stands with his sword, facing a distant black tower — *"Instead, he stood."*
7. He walks out through the broken gates toward the tower — *"And the road to take it back began."*

Colour arc: gold → cold blue storm → grey rain → warm dawn (last frame mirrors the first).
Music arc: warm strings and horns → dark low strings and drums → lone cello in the rain → brass build at sunrise.

### Title card
**THE LAST CROWN**, on a big orchestral hit.

### 0:45 — Chapter I: The Road Home (gameplay)
- The last painting breaks into pixels and becomes a full-screen 8-bit level.
- On screen: **OBJECTIVE: REACH THE SHADOW TOWER**.
- The pixel king runs right: jumps a pit, collects sunstones, hits a crown block, stomps a shadow creature, climbs the stairs, touches the banner at the tower gate.
- HUD: score, sunstone count, timer. Chiptune music; jump, coin and stomp sounds.
- Ends on **CHAPTER CLEAR**.

## 5. How it was made
- **AI-generated:** the paintings, George's narration, the orchestral score.
- **Made in code:** the anchor spin, the game, its chiptune music and sound effects.
- All original — no Nintendo characters, blocks or music.
